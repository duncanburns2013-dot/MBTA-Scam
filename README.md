## MBTA Crisis — Key Findings

- **Pension Fund:** Only 52.6% funded — $1.62 billion unfunded liability
- **Operating Deficit:** $473M–$700M annually as federal COVID aid expires
- **Capital Gap:** $13 billion infrastructure backlog
- **Big Dig Debt:** $8B+ in highway debt dumped on a transit agency
- **EY Consultant Scandal:** $610/hour consultants hired to "research" what the Advisory Board already documented
- **Combined Shortfall:** ~$1.1B/year = $412 per MA household

## Data Sources

All data comes from official U.S. government and public sources:

- IRS Statistics of Income (migration data, AGI shares)
- Bureau of Economic Analysis (Regional Price Parities, Personal Income)
- Bureau of Labor Statistics (CPI, wages)
- U.S. Census Bureau (ACS, population)
- Energy Information Administration (electricity rates)
- Tax Foundation (State Tax Competitiveness Index)
- MA Department of Revenue
- MA Division of Local Services
- MBTA Advisory Board
- MBTA Retirement Fund Annual Reports
- National Transit Database
- MA State Auditor / Bureau of Special Investigations
- Boston Globe (EY contract investigation)

## Setup

No build tools required. These are standalone HTML files with Chart.js loaded from CDN.

1. Clone the repo
2. Enable GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Source: main branch)
3. Your dashboards are live
